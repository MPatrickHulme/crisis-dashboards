Reading the figure. Left axis: Congressional Sentiment Score — the mean per-lawmaker position on U.S. military force, −0.5 oppose to +0.5 support, from a three-model ensemble (Grok, Luna, Gemini) over relevant statements. Solid line, tripartite instrument; dashed, 8-item. Estimated on days with ≥3 speaking members, each weighted equally. Periods below that threshold are interpolated, and the shaded band is the distance from zero. Right axis: share voting to support force on contemporaneous roll calls — the Yea share on authorising measures, the Nay share on restricting ones — so both axes read support-upward and CSS 0 meets the 50% line. Purple markers are chamber totals (diamond, Senate; square, House); small red and blue diamonds are the party shares; a gold halo marks a measure that carried. Hover a marker for the tally and sources. Lower panel: daily statement volume by party. Media strip: share of statements by source.
