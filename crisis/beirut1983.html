Reading the figure. Both axes read support-up and CSS 0 aligns with the 50% line, so votes and sentiment are directly comparable. Large purple markers = whole chamber (diamond = Senate, square = House); small red/blue diamonds on the same stem = Republican and Democratic shares; a gold halo marks measures that carried. Hover any marker for the bill, tally and party split with links to the sources (voteview covers every Congress; congress.gov where coverage exists); click a marker to open the primary source.
